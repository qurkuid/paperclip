# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Paperclip is used by operators and board-level owners who coordinate autonomous agents and need to understand company state quickly. They scan work, approvals, budgets, performance, and outputs from a desktop or phone, then decide what needs attention and what action to take next.

For the Spacebogam company, the primary user is the business owner reviewing marketing and consultation performance between daily operational tasks. The interface must support a confident decision within seconds without requiring analytics expertise.

## Product Purpose

Paperclip is the control plane for autonomous AI companies. It connects goals, agents, tasks, costs, approvals, and business outcomes so every action remains inspectable and tied to a company objective.

Success means the top layer answers three questions in order:

1. What is happening?
2. Does it need me?
3. What should I do next?

Detailed evidence, raw logs, and technical state remain available through progressive disclosure, but they do not lead the experience.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, calm, operational.

Paperclip should feel like a trusted chief of staff: concise enough for fast scanning, rigorous enough to explain every recommendation, and restrained enough that status and risk carry the visual emphasis.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards made from interchangeable metric cards.
- Decorative AI aesthetics, gradient-heavy surfaces, glass panels, and ornamental motion.
- Analytics tools that expose charts without explaining the decision they support.
- Raw logs, provider configuration, or technical plumbing presented as the primary view.
- Dense enterprise administration that makes routine decisions feel bureaucratic.

## Design Principles

1. **Decision before detail.** Lead with the current judgment, the evidence behind it, and the next action.
2. **Company state over software state.** Describe business outcomes in the operator's language, not implementation language.
3. **Evidence earns confidence.** Recommendations show sample quality, comparison basis, and the metric that would prove improvement.
4. **Progressive disclosure preserves speed.** Summaries lead; tables and technical details remain available when needed.
5. **Every output is inspectable.** A user can trace a claim to the data, task, artifact, or agent responsible for it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for core product flows. Meaning must never depend on color alone. Interactive states require visible focus, keyboard support, descriptive labels, and touch targets suitable for mobile use. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences and remains limited to state communication.
